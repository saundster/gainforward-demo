New: the pill row only appears when there's more than one active journey (already possible today for a mentor with several mentees, not just dual-role). Clicking a pill swaps everything below — stage tracker, log, upcoming meetings — to that journey. Today there's no switcher at all; a second journey is mentioned in one sentence of subtext and otherwise invisible.

What has to change under the hood

  • Role becomes two independent flags, not one field. preferredFormat is currently a single string that each signup form overwrites — becoming a mentee today silently erases mentor status. Track isMentor / isMentee separately so completing one never clears the other.
  • A journey records its own roles at creation time. Rather than asking "what's this person's current preferredFormat" every time (which can drift after the fact), each journey stores who was mentor and who was mentee the moment it formed.
  • Capacity splits by role. Mentor-side capacity (menteeCapacity) and the 1-active-mentee-relationship-at-a-time rule are tracked independently, so being mentored doesn't eat into your own capacity as a mentor.
  • Matching becomes direction-aware. Directory needs to know which role you're currently filling ("looking for a mentor" vs. "looking for a mentee") to score and filter candidates correctly — a dual-role person's card shows a chip for each role they hold.
  • Every screen keyed to "the" journey gets keyed to "the selected" journey. ~15 places in the code call a function that assumes exactly one active journey; those all move to reading whichever journey the switcher currently has selected.
