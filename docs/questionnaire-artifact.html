Participant Profile Questionnaire

The profile every participant completes to join GainForward — once at sign-up, and any time after to update.

There is one form for everyone: mentors and mentees answer the same questions. What differs is emphasis — a mentor leans on "what you can offer," a mentee on "what you want to learn" — and the one format question below, not a separate track. This mirrors the pilot's skill-first design: expertise and curiosity matter more than title or tenure.

In the product, this is the mandatory setup screen shown the first time someone opens GainForward, and the "My profile" screen from then on.
